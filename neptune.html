Neptune

Neptune is the most distant planet from the Sun and an ice giant with supersonic winds. Its deep-blue atmosphere holds fast-moving storms, and its large moon Triton orbits backward.

A faraway world

Neptune receives very little sunlight, yet its atmosphere is active, with clouds and storms driven by internal heat. Winds can reach more than 2,000 km/h (1,200 mph).

Neptune's largest moon, Triton, is geologically active and travels around the planet in a retrograde orbit. Voyager 2 made the first and only close flyby of Neptune in 1989.

One orbit takes Neptune about 165 Earth years; it completed its first full orbit since its 1846 discovery in 2011.
