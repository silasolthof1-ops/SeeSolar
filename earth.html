Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. Liquid water covers about 71% of its surface, and a protective atmosphere supports a remarkable range of ecosystems.

Our living world

Earth's atmosphere is mostly nitrogen and oxygen. Its magnetic field helps shield the surface from charged particles from the Sun, while the planet's orbit and climate keep abundant surface water liquid. These circumstances allow life to flourish on its surface.

Earth formed about 4.5 billion years ago. Continents shift as tectonic plates move, reshaping the surface over geologic time. The Moon's gravity drives ocean tides and helps stabilize Earth's axial tilt.

Earth is the only planet whose English name does not come from a Greek or Roman deity.
