Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet, a gas giant with colorful cloud bands and storms. Its Great Red Spot is a giant storm that has raged for centuries, and its moon Europa may hide a subsurface ocean.

A giant world

Jupiter is more than twice as massive as all the other planets combined. It has no solid surface; its atmosphere becomes denser and hotter deeper inside, above a likely core of heavy elements.

Jupiter's four largest moons—Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto—were first observed by Galileo Galilei in 1610. Ganymede is larger than Mercury, and Europa is a leading target in the search for habitable environments.

Jupiter completes one rotation in about 10 hours, giving it the shortest day of any planet.
