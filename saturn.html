Saturn

Saturn is a gas giant famous for its vast, bright ring system. The rings are made of countless pieces of ice and rock, from tiny grains to chunks as large as mountains.

A decorated world

Saturn's rings stretch far from the planet but are remarkably thin. The planet itself is mostly hydrogen and helium, with powerful winds and a distinctive six-sided jet stream near its north pole.

Saturn's moon Titan has rivers and lakes of liquid methane, while icy Enceladus sprays water-rich plumes into space. Both moons are important destinations for future exploration.

Saturn is the least dense planet; its average density is lower than water's.
