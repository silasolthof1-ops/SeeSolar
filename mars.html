Mars

Mars is a cold desert world with a thin atmosphere and a rusty-red surface. Its ancient river valleys and polar ice caps preserve clues that liquid water once flowed across the planet.

A studied world

At roughly half Earth's diameter, Mars has seasons, weather, volcanoes, and the largest canyon in the solar system. Its two tiny moons, Phobos and Deimos, may be captured asteroids.

Rovers and orbiters study the planet's geology and climate, and search for signs of ancient microbial life. NASA's Perseverance rover is collecting samples for possible future return to Earth.

Olympus Mons rises about 22 km (13.6 mi) above the surrounding plains—nearly three times the height of Mount Everest above sea level.
