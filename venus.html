Venus

Venus is a rocky planet wrapped in thick clouds of sulfuric acid. Its powerful greenhouse atmosphere traps heat, making it the hottest planet even though Mercury is closer to the Sun.

A hidden world

The atmosphere at Venus's surface is about 90 times as dense as Earth's, with temperatures around 465°C (870°F). Its clouds hide a landscape of volcanic plains, mountains, and impact craters.

Venus rotates very slowly and in the opposite direction from most planets. A single rotation takes about 243 Earth days—longer than its 225-day trip around the Sun.

A day on Venus, measured from one sunrise to the next, is about 117 Earth days.
